## Hi! I am [Yiu-Fai (Frankie) CHEUNG 張耀輝](https://iafcy.github.io/). 👋🏻

I am a first-year M.Phil. student in [Department of Computer Science and Engineering](https://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/) at the Chinese University of Hong Kong ([CUHK](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/index.html)), advised by Prof. [Irwin King](https://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/irwin.king/).

I received my Bachelor's degree with First Class Honors in Computer Science at [CUHK](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/index.html), as a member of the [ELITE Stream](https://www.erg.cuhk.edu.hk/erg/Elite) of the Faculty of Engineering.

My research interest includes Large Language Models, AI Agents and Multi-Agent Systems.

I feel very fortunate to have the opportunity to work under the supervision of Prof. [Wenxuan Wang](https://jarviswang94.github.io/) at [Renmin University of China](https://en.ruc.edu.cn/) and Prof. [Hao Wang](https://intellisys.haow.us/haowang/) at [Stevens Institute of Technology](https://www.stevens.edu/).
